BiographyThe man. The curls. The legend.

Marwan Fattal is a Lebanese young man and proud son of the Fattal family of Tripoli, a family whose name has been respected in the North for generations. He is the son of Afif Fattal.

From a very young age, Marwan showed the signs that something was different. While other kids asked for toys, Marwan asked for business plans. While others followed trends, Marwan was busy becoming one.

Today, he is widely known among his friends for three things: his legendary curls, his unbeatable confidence, and the fact that he is the only one of them who is actually on Google.

His plan is simple: become a self-made millionaire, then a billionaire, then buy the whole street. In that order.
