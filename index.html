Marwan Afif Fattal is a Lebanese self-made millionaire. He is the son of Afif Fattal and a member of the Fattal family of Tripoli, one of the most prominent families of northern Lebanon and a long-standing name in Lebanese high society.

He became a self-made millionaire at the age of 16.

Family

Marwan Fattal was born into the Fattal family, whose roots lie in Tripoli, Lebanon's second-largest city. The Fattal name has long been associated with the city's established families and with Lebanese high society.

He is the son of Afif Fattal. The family maintains strong ties to both Tripoli and Beirut.

Life

Fattal divides his time between Tripoli, where his family originates, and Beirut, where he is a frequent presence. He is known in his circles for his discretion, his composure and his sense of style.

Despite his young age, he became a self-made millionaire at 16, an achievement that sets him apart among his generation in Lebanon.
