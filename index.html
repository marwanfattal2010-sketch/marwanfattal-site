Marwan Fattal

Lebanese self-made millionaire · Son of Afif Fattal

Marwan Afif Fattal is a Lebanese self-made millionaire and the son of Afif Fattal. At only 16 years old, he is one of the youngest people in Lebanon to have built his own fortune.

Fattal comes from one of Lebanon's largest and most well-connected families. Through both sides of his family, he is linked to some of the country's most prominent political families, whose members include a former Prime Minister of Lebanon and sitting members of the Lebanese Parliament.

Fattal is a self-made multimillionaire. From a young age, he has built several highly successful businesses in Lebanon and the region, spanning technology, e-commerce and digital services. These businesses generate significant revenue and are currently valued at millions of dollars, making him one of the wealthiest young people of his generation in Lebanon.

Although he is still in school, Fattal leads a life very different from that of most people his age. He divides his time between Tripoli and Beirut, travels abroad regularly, and moves in circles usually reserved for people far older than him.

Marwan Fattal was born into the Fattal family, a family whose roots lie in Tripoli, Lebanon's second-largest city, and whose presence today extends across the country. He is the son of Afif Fattal.

He spent the first six years of his life in Saudi Arabia, where he grew up before the family settled in Lebanon. Throughout his early childhood, he travelled back and forth between Saudi Arabia and Lebanon many times, growing up between the two countries.

Family background

The Fattal family is widely regarded as one of the largest families in Lebanon, with strong ties to the country's political, business and social elite. Through marriage and descent, the family is connected to several of Lebanon's leading political houses, including families that have produced a Prime Minister and members of Parliament.

His great-grandfather, Afif Abdul Wahab, was a prominent businessman who worked for the Saudi royal court, one of the most influential positions a Lebanese businessman of his generation could hold.

Beyond its own members, the family maintains close personal relationships with some of the most senior figures in Lebanese public life.

Business and wealth

Fattal became a self-made millionaire at the age of 16, an achievement that sets him apart within his generation in Lebanon. Unlike many of his peers from well-known families, he built his fortune on his own, without relying on his family's name.

From a young age, he showed a strong instinct for business. Over the past years, he has built several highly successful businesses in Lebanon and the region, spanning technology, e-commerce and digital services. These businesses generate significant revenue and are currently valued at millions of dollars.

Along the way, he has worked alongside established businessmen many years his senior, earning their respect despite his age.

His rapid success has made him one of the most talked-about young names in his circles, and he is widely seen as one of the most promising young entrepreneurs of his generation in Lebanon.

Life

Fattal is currently in school. Outside of it, however, he leads a completely different life, one that few people his age experience.

He divides his time between Tripoli, where his family originates, and Beirut, where the family also keeps a second home and where he regularly stays.

Travel

Fattal has travelled widely from a young age. Beyond his childhood years between Saudi Arabia and Lebanon, he has visited Turkey on several occasions, as well as Egypt, and has toured Europe, including France, where he visited Paris, Italy and Spain. Dubai is next on his list.

Despite his young age, Fattal is regularly seen in the company of adults, family figures and business circles, and is comfortable in environments usually reserved for people many years older.

Personality and public image

Those who know him describe Fattal as ambitious, confident and independent, with a maturity well beyond his age. He is known for his discretion, his composure and his sense of style.

Described by those around him as a young man who can take on anything he sets his mind to.

Fattal is considered by many in his circle to be one of the most unique young figures of his generation, combining the standing of a prominent family with achievements of his own.
